Call-out questions

What electricians want pinned down first

How does it decide what is urgent?

By following the rules you set with Andy and asking the questions you have chosen. When the answers match what you have called urgent, it reaches you straight away. You also decide in advance which way it should lean when it is unsure.

What does it tell someone in danger?

Anyone reporting burning smells, sparking or danger is told to leave the property and call 999, or the network operator on 105 for a power cut. It gives no electrical advice and makes no attempt to diagnose the fault.

Can it take my out-of-hours rates?

Yes, if you want callers to hear them. Give Andy your call-out rates and the hours they apply, and it can state them as given. If you would sooner quote yourself, it says the price will be confirmed.

Do I still get the routine enquiries?

Yes. Routine work is logged with its details and kept for the morning, so none of it disappears because it came in at night.

How is this different from aiforelectricians.co.uk?

aiforelectricians.co.uk looks at AI and automation across a whole electrical business. This site deals with one thing only, which is emergency and out-of-hours call-outs.

Is it an answering service for electricians?

It does what an electrician answering service would, then adds the sorting by your rules. Some people would call it an AI receptionist for electricians. Whatever the name, callers are told it is an AI.
